01 / 07

Direction study

Three interpretations of “quiet technology”. The archive-facing Editorial Instrument direction is selected for implementation.

A / MONOLITH01

Cold Museum

White space, graphite type, object as sculpture. Maximum quiet, minimum warmth; loses the felt, collectible quality of paper.

SELECTED
B / INSTRUMENT02

Editorial Instrument

Warm black, foil, measured type and technical rules. Reads like a publisher’s archive crossed with a precision instrument.

C / FIBRE03

Living Archive

Oxide red, exposed fibre and tactile grain. Emotional and organic, but less able to carry the benchmark and deployment chapters.

Decision: B keeps the physical-object story legible across all six chapters and gives metric/CTA components a stable, high-contrast home.
